Patent record

Disclosed herein is a MEMS sensor package that includes a substrate, an annular-shaped first dry film pattern stuck to one surface of the substrate, and a MEMS sensor chip including a tubular support and a detection part which is supported on the support so as to overlap a cavity of the support. The MEMS sensor chip is fixed to the substrate by sticking an annular mounting surface of the support to the first dry film pattern.

Historic Invention

Cinematograph

Associated inventor: Auguste and Louis Lumière

A cinematograph or kinematograph was an early motion picture film mechanism of various kinds. The name was originally used for machines that combined the functions of a movie camera and of a film projector, and in the case of the Cinématographe Lumière, also means to print films from the negative. The term would also be used for separate devices, and became the root for the now more common terms "cinematography" and "cinema".
